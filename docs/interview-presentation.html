The Operational Silo Problem

Contesting a single payment dispute requires manually pulling data across 3 to 5 disconnected SaaS platforms:

  • Shopify / WooCommerce: Customer order details, account tenure, prior orders.
  • Stripe / Gateways: Payment tokens, AVS street/zip match, CVC check, 3DS authentication.
  • ShipStation / Carriers: Carrier tracking events, delivery scans, signed POD receipts.
  • Zendesk / Gorgias: Customer support transcripts, replacement offers, policy terms.
The Polarized Market Trap
  • Option A: Manual Representation: Unstructured screenshot dumps into gateway portals. Slow (45 min), high staff cost, and issuing banks reject unstructured files (<20% win rate [S-06]).
  • Option B: Autopilot Contingency Bots: Vendors like Chargeflow charge an advertised 25% contingency take-rate on gross funds recovered [S-11].
  • The Core Tension: On high-ticket transactions ($150–$1,500), paying 25% costs $75 to $375 per claim, severely penalizing merchants on thin 10%–15% net retail margins.

The Contextual Evidence Gap on Merchandise Claims (Reason Code 4853)

Automated bots submit standard carrier tracking on autopilot. However, on Mastercard Reason Code 4853 (Defective Merchandise / Not as Described), carrier tracking alone is insufficient. Under network standards, issuers require proof of the merchant's published return policy and evidence that a remedy (replacement parts or repair) was offered. When bots omit these contextual exhibits, issuers reject the claim, causing a permanent financial loss.

Slide 3 of 6 · Product Architecture

ChargeDossier: A Deterministic Evidence Review Copilot

Replacing black-box bots and hallucinatory LLMs with an explainable, deterministic evidence auditor and human approval checkpoint for $49/month.

~55s
Phase 1 · Multi-Source Harvest
1. Ingest & Inspect

Extracts records across 4 dedicated inspection tabs: Order Details, Payment Authentication tokens (AVS, CVC, 3DS), Carrier Fulfillment (POD & signature), and Policy acceptance transcripts.

Phase 2 · Deterministic Engine
2. Heuristic Audit & Fix

Evaluates evidence completeness (0–100 score) against Mastercard Reason Codes 4837 and 4853. Catches fatal flaws (in-transit delay) and surfaces interactive remediation for missing policies.

Phase 3 · Structured Lock
3. Compile & Decide

Compiles an illustrative Second Presentment Dossier with Exhibits A through E modeled on Mastercom v6 layouts (<18 pages [S-01]). Operator explicitly Approves or Rejects to accept loss.

Why Deterministic Rules Over Generative AI?
  • Zero Hallucination Tolerance: Second presentment is an adversarial quasi-legal process. If an LLM misquotes a tracking timestamp or fabricates a scheme rule, the merchant suffers immediate loss and pre-arbitration risks.
  • 100% Mathematical Explainability: Every point awarded (+15 AVS, +10 CVC, +35 Fulfillment) and deducted (-25 In-Transit, -10 Missing Policy) is fully auditable.
  • Sub-5ms Execution: Executes locally with zero API token costs or latency.
Why Retain Human Review? (Copilot vs. Autopilot)
  • High-Ticket Judgment: For a $1,150 order, spending 60 to 90 seconds reviewing exhibits protects substantial capital.
  • Avoid Wasted Staff Effort: Unwinnable cases (e.g. carrier still delayed in transit) are flagged immediately as Tier C, enabling operators to accept loss early.
  • Decision Integrity: Machine automation gathers data and formats exhibits; human operators retain strategic approval authority.
