Market Reality & Root Cause

The Three Evidence Failure Modes in High-Ticket Disputes

Why online merchants write off winnable disputes and why blind submission causes permanent losses.

03 / 12
Failure Mode A MISSING

Absent Critical Exhibits

Under scheme guidelines, disputes require specific nexus proofs. On Reason Code 4853 (Merchandise) claims, submitting tracking without attaching the store's published return policy or customer support remedy results in automatic issuing bank rejection.

  • Missing timestamped checkout terms acceptance
  • Absent carrier signature receipt on orders > $150
  • No documented repair or replacement offer transcript
Failure Mode B CONTRADICTORY

Self-Defeating Evidence

Automated bots ingest data without semantic cross-checks. Submitting carrier tracking showing a package is still "In Transit" (weather delay) or an order delivered to a Florida freight forwarder with failed AVS guarantees rejection while wasting administrative fees.

  • Package delivered to unverified forwarder destination
  • Support transcript contains cardholder theft claim
  • Billing state mismatch with failed AVS street match ('N')
Failure Mode C IRRELEVANT

Scheme Mismatch

Evidence that defeats one reason code is useless for another. Submitting terms of service to counter Reason Code 4837 (Fraud / No Authorization) fails because cardholders did not authorize checkout. Only 3DS tokens, AVS/CVC matches, and device IP establish liability shift.

  • Survey: ~60% of merchants abandon 40%+ chargebacks [S-15]
  • Manual fraud win rate averages under 20% [S-06]
  • LexisNexis: $1 fraud costs $4.61–$5.13 in total friction [S-05]

Architectural Principles

Why Deterministic Math Beats Generative AI

In financial dispute representment, submitting fabricated or hallucinated statements to issuing banks risks scheme fines and acquiring account termination. ChargeDossier rejects black-box LLMs in favor of strict deterministic math.

  • 100% Mathematical Auditability: Every rule assigns verifiable points and reasons based on published scheme standards.
  • Zero Hallucination Risk: The engine never invents customer quotes, fake tracking numbers, or unrecorded policies.
  • Early Loss Acceptance as a Win: Rejecting unwinnable claims saves operator time and avoids wasting staff hours on unviable submissions.
  • Explicit Status: This thesis is an exploratory product hypothesis, not a confirmed commercial outcome.
Epistemic Boundary: Hypothesis to be tested in Phase 2B merchant discovery sprint ($n=8$) Independent portfolio project. Not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Mastercard.

Scoring Algorithm

The Deterministic Decision Framework

Actual point allocations, contradiction penalties, and quality tier boundaries from src/engine.js.

07 / 12
Gross Scoring (100 Pts)

Heuristic Rule Allocations

  • Payment & Identity (30 pts): AVS street/zip match (12 pts), CVC match (8 pts), 3DS authentication (10 pts).
  • Carrier POD & Tracking (35 pts): Delivered status (15 pts), destination match (10 pts), cardholder signature on ≥ $150 (10 pts).
  • Policies & Disclosures (15 pts): Terms accepted at checkout (8 pts), published return policy attached (7 pts).
  • First-Party Trust (20 pts): Prior orders & account tenure (10 pts), customer communication/remedy logs (10 pts).
Contradiction Deductions

Fatal Evidentiary Flaws

  • Delivery Destination Mismatch: Package delivered to different address → -20 pts (Critical)
  • Third-Party Signature: Signed by freight forwarder, not customer → -10 pts (Warning)
  • Explicit Fraud Affirmation: Cardholder stated card was stolen → -25 pts (Critical)
  • Defect Without Remedy: Merchandise defect without repair offer → -10 pts (Warning)
  • State Mismatch + Failed AVS: Billing state ≠ Shipping with AVS 'N' → -20 pts (Critical)
Decision Tiers

Explainable Recommendations

  • TIER A (80–100 Pts): Strong evidence, zero contradictions, zero missing items → APPROVE_FOR_PRESENTMENT
  • TIER B (60–79 Pts): Fixable gaps detected, no critical contradictions → REVIEW_AND_ATTACH_EVIDENCE
  • TIER C (0–59 Pts): Deficient evidence or critical contradictions → REJECT_AND_ACCEPT_LOSS
Net = Gross - Penalties
Strict Mathematical Formula
