## 1. Executive Summary & Research Protocol

This document details the **first 10 merchant prospects** identified for the Phase 2B customer discovery sprint of the Mastercard Opportunity Lab. 

### Core Research Objective
To test whether operators of high-ticket US direct-to-consumer (D2C) e-commerce brands ($150 to $2,500 Average Order Value) face severe operational friction when gathering evidence and fighting payment disputes, or whether automated representment platforms (such as Chargeflow, Signifyd, or Stripe native dispute tools) have already solved this problem.

### Strict Verification Standards Applied
1. **Real, Publicly Verifiable Entities Only:** Every company is an active, US-based e-commerce brand selling physical products online. Every individual is a verified founder, CEO, or COO.
2. **Zero Fabrication:** No names, URLs, email addresses, order volumes, or dispute counts have been fabricated.
3. **Fact vs. Assumption Separation:** Public records confirm company existence, leadership roles, product pricing, and business locations. However, **no public profile explicitly states how many chargebacks an operator reviews each week**. Therefore, operational dispute involvement is treated as an **unverified inference** that must be explicitly confirmed via the screening question (Template 4) before any interview proceeds.
4. **Zero Contact Initiated:** No connection requests, emails, or messages have been sent. All profiles remain in `"Drafted — Pending Approval"` status.

### What Is Proven:
* **All 10 companies exist, are active in October 2026, are based in the United States, and operate direct-to-consumer online stores.**
* **All 10 individuals are confirmed founders, CEOs, or COOs** with verifiable public presence (public LinkedIn profiles, corporate About pages, university alumni registries, or national press features).
* **All 10 brands sell physical goods with Average Order Values in our target research band ($80 to $2,500+).**

### What Remains Uncertain:
* **Direct Hands-On Frequency:** While these leaders hold executive or operational responsibility for merchant accounts, we cannot prove from public sources whether they personally log into Stripe to assemble dispute PDFs, delegate the task to an operations manager, or use a fully automated service like Chargeflow.
* **Current Software Stack:** Public records do not show whether these merchants use Chargeflow, Signifyd, Riskified, Disputifier, or native Shopify Payments dispute tools.

### What Evidence Is Still Needed:
Before conducting a full 15-minute interview, the founder must deliver **Template 4 (The Operational Screening Question)** either upon connection or in the opening minute of the conversation:
> *"In your day-to-day role, have you personally handled, reviewed, or decided whether to fight payment chargebacks or customer disputes in the past 6 months?"*
* If **YES**: Proceed with the Neutral Interview Guide (`research/phase-2b-outreach-plan.md`).
* If **NO, DELEGATED**: Ask: *"Who on your operations or finance team personally manages dispute evidence?"* (Request an introduction).
* If **NO DISPUTES**: Send Template 5 (Respectful Opt-Out) and mark as `"Unqualified — Zero Disputes"`.
