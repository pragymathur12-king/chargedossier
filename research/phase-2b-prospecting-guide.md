## 1. Overview & Objectives

This guide is a practical, beginner-friendly manual for finding and recruiting **US e-commerce operators** who handle payment disputes for high-ticket products ($150 to $2,000 Average Order Value).

### Core Principles:
* **Zero Paid Tools Required:** Uses standard free LinkedIn search and public Google search operators.
* **Radical Transparency:** You are an Integrated MBA student conducting academic research for your master's thesis and product discovery portfolio. You are **not selling software or consulting**.
* **Zero Fabrication:** Never invent prospect details, job titles, or responses.
* **Strict Confidentiality:** Never ask for or record customer credit card numbers (PANs), cardholder names, or proprietary merchant banking credentials.

### Step 1: How to Find Suitable US High-Ticket E-Commerce Brands (Free Google Search)
We are targeting direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands selling physical goods where dispute costs and chargeback stakes are high:
* **Target Categories:** Consumer electronics, specialized audio gear, fine jewelry, boutique furniture, high-end sporting equipment (e-bikes, golf, scuba), and specialist tools.
* **Search Queries (Run directly in standard Google):**
  1. `site:myshopify.com "headphones" "$300" "cart"`
  2. `site:myshopify.com "jewelry" "$500" "add to cart"`
  3. `site:myshopify.com "furniture" "$800" "checkout"`
  4. `"powered by shopify" "Austin, TX" OR "California" "electronics" "$250"`
  5. `inurl:collections "espresso machine" "$600" "USD"`
* **How to Check the Store in 30 Seconds:**
  * Open the store website.
  * Check the catalog: Are products priced **above $150–$200**? (If products are $20 T-shirts, skip).
  * Check the footer: Is it a US-based business with US shipping addresses?

---

### Step 2: How to Identify the Exact Operator on Free LinkedIn
Once you have the store brand name (e.g., *AeroSound Audio*), find the person who handles payment operations:
* **Target Job Titles by Company Size:**
  * *Small Brands (1–10 employees):* **Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, COO, or Head of E-Commerce**. (In small stores, the founder or COO personally reviews chargebacks in Shopify/Stripe).
  * *Mid-Sized Brands (10–50 employees):* **Head of Operations, Operations Manager, Director of Customer Experience, Payment Operations Lead, or Risk & Fraud Specialist**.
* **Free LinkedIn Search Syntax (in the standard LinkedIn search bar):**
  * Search: `"[Brand Name]" AND ("Operations" OR "E-commerce" OR "Founder" OR "Payments")`
  * Filter by **People** $\rightarrow$ Location: **United States**.
* **Look at the Experience Section:**
  * Does their profile mention managing Shopify, Stripe, order fulfillment, merchant operations, or customer service?

## 3. Method for Recording Public Source Evidence

To maintain institutional research credibility, every row in your outreach tracker must link to a **publicly verifiable source** proving why this person is relevant.

### Acceptable Public Sources & How to Record Them:
1. **Public LinkedIn Profile Summary / Experience:**
   * *Example Notation:* `"LinkedIn profile experience bullet states: 'Managed daily e-commerce operations, Stripe payment integrations, and merchant chargeback rebuttals across Shopify Plus.' [URL]"`
2. **Company Website 'About Us' or 'Team' Page:**
   * *Example Notation:* `"Official store website team page lists Jane Doe as Co-Founder & Head of Operations: store.com/about-us [URL]"`
3. **Public E-Commerce Community Post or Podcast Interview:**
   * *Example Notation:* `"Speaker on e-commerce podcast discussing managing return fraud and dispute ratios for luxury luggage: podcast.com/episode-42 [URL]"`

## 4. The Five Outreach & Communication Templates

Use these exact word-for-word templates. They are designed to be courteous, honest, and completely devoid of sales jargon.

### Template 1: Transparent LinkedIn Connection Message (<300 Characters)
> *"Hi [First Name], I’m an Integrated MBA student conducting academic research on how US high-ticket e-commerce brands manage payment dispute workflows and evidence collection. Not selling anything—just seeking 15 minutes of operational perspective for my master's case study. Would love to connect!"*

---

### Template 2: Concise Interview Invitation (Sent After Connection Accepted)
> *"Hi [First Name], thank you for connecting!  
>  
> I am conducting academic discovery research on the operational friction e-commerce operators face when gathering evidence for expensive payment disputes (chargebacks over $150).  
>  
> Given your role overseeing operations at [Company Name], I would be immensely grateful for a brief, 15-minute confidential conversation about your past experience with dispute responses and chargeback tools.  
>  
> I am not selling software or consulting—this is purely for my graduate fintech research. To respect your time, I am glad to share our completed payments industry benchmarking report once finished.  
>  
> Would you have 15 minutes open this Thursday or Friday afternoon for a quick call?  
>  
> Best regards,  
> [Your Name]  
> Integrated MBA Candidate (Class of 2027)"*

---

### Template 3: One Polite Follow-Up (Sent 3 to 4 Days Later if No Reply)
> *"Hi [First Name], following up briefly on my previous note. I know you are extraordinarily busy managing store operations at [Company Name].  
>  
> If you have 10–15 minutes this week to share your past experience handling chargebacks, it would be an enormous help to my academic study. If your schedule is completely packed, I completely understand and thank you for your time!  
>  
> Best regards,  
> [Your Name]"*

---

### Template 4: Short Screening Message (Before or at the Start of the Call)
*Use this message to confirm they have personally handled disputes before diving into the interview:*
> *"Hi [First Name], before our chat, I wanted to quickly confirm: in your day-to-day role at [Company Name], have you personally handled, reviewed, or decided whether to fight payment chargebacks or customer disputes in the past 6 months? (I want to ensure our research focuses strictly on hands-on operational experiences!)"*

---

### Template 5: Respectful Opt-Out / Graceful Close Message
*Send this if a prospect declines or states they do not handle disputes:*
> *"Thank you for letting me know, [First Name]! I appreciate your quick reply and completely understand. Wishing you and the [Company Name] team continued success this quarter.  
>  
> Best regards,  
> [Your Name]"*

### Safety Rules on Free LinkedIn:
* **Never exceed 15 connection requests per day** on a standard free LinkedIn account to avoid automated spam restrictions.
* **Always include a personalized note** (Template 1). Generic blank connection requests have a <15% acceptance rate; student research notes average 35%–50%.
* **Never pitch a solution in the first message.**
