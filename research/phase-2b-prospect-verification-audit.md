## 1. Audit Purpose & Methodological Standards

This document is an independent, skeptical verification audit of the candidate pool for the Phase 2B merchant discovery sprint. 

### Critical Terminology & Epistemic Corrections:
1. **Catalog Product Price Range $\neq$ Average Order Value (AOV):** A direct-to-consumer store may sell a flagship item for $350, but its historical Average Order Value (AOV) depends on multi-item baskets, promotional discounts, and lower-priced accessories. Because external observers cannot access private merchant transaction databases without authorization, **true AOV is classified as Unverified / Unknown** for all candidates. Only public catalog price ranges are verified.
2. **No False "Fully Verified" Labels:** A candidate or merchant is **not** labeled "fully verified" when material claims—specifically their hands-on chargeback workflow, dispute volume, or tool dissatisfaction—remain unsupported.
3. **Independent Assessment Across Five Dimensions:** Every candidate is audited across five isolated pillars:
   * **Company Location** (Verified Fact vs. Unverified)
   * **Current Professional Role** (Verified Fact vs. Unverified)
   * **Catalog Product Price Range** (Verified Fact vs. Unverified)
   * **Estimated Team Size** (Estimated / Unverified exact headcount)
   * **Hands-on Chargeback Responsibility** (Verified Fact vs. Unverified Inference)
4. **Ten Distinct Companies (Zero Duplicates):** In accordance with discovery best practices, the duplicate candidates from Nomad Goods and Branch Furniture have been resolved by retaining the single most relevant candidate from each and adding verified replacements from distinct US e-commerce brands.
5. **Ranking by Accessibility and Evidence Quality:** Candidates are ranked by empirical evidence transparency and plausible accessibility for a 15-minute student academic interview, rather than brand prestige or gross revenue.
